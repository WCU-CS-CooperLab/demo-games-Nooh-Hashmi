# Project 3 Changes

## Change 1: Green Rings
this first change is pretty simple, there is a 5% chance for a green ring to spawn. This green ring give the player double points and double the amount of fuel that the regular orange rings would give you. The Green Rings spawn at level 2

## Change 2: incrimental speed
as you go further and further into the level, the faster your plane goes. each level incriments the planes forward speed by the level * 2

## Change 3: White Orbs
White orbs spawn in the chunk at level 2 when the green rings spawn. the White orbs if hit, decrease your fuel by 2.
